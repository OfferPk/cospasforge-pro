# COSPASForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

Emergency satellite beacons are mandated across global air transport (over 28,000 commercial passenger aircraft subject to ICAO GADSS mandates) and commercial shipping (over 100,000 SOLAS-compliant vessels). Dedicated hardware beacon test receivers and RF decoders (WS Technologies, Sartech, Aeroflex, Cobham) cost \$15,000 to \$35,000 per laboratory unit. COSPASForge Pro provides an accessible, browser-native development, simulation, and compliance verification environment for SAR avionics engineers, maritime electronics certifying bodies, and search-and-rescue ground station developers.
